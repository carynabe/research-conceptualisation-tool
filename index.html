<div class="masthead">

  <h1>Masters research tools</h1>

  <p>Four tools to develop the thinking behind your research proposal. Work through them in order. Each tool builds on and stress-tests what the previous one produced.</p>

</div>

<div class="process-note"><strong>On thinking order versus writing order:</strong> Your proposal is read in the order: purpose → problem → questions → literature → design. But it is developed differently — you discover your PPSQ through working with the literature, and you justify your design through working with your questions. These tools follow the thinking order. The writing order comes after.</div>
